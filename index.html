    Dear Parents,

    At Sloka International School, Manneguda, we believe every child is born with a unique spark. Our role is to help that spark grow into confidence, curiosity and character.

    For seven years, we have delivered the Cambridge curriculum from Early Years to AS & A Levels. We focus on understanding rather than memorising. Through AI classes, Biz Talk, Tiny Talkers, Dealathon and adventure sports, they gain skills for a fast-changing world.
